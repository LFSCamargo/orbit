Orbit has always shipped quickly: twelve releases since July, each with a long list of
changes in `CHANGELOG.md`. That list is useful when you already know what you are looking
for, but it doesn't tell the story behind a release, and it was easy to miss when Orbit
gained something big.

So Orbit now has a blog.

## What you will find here

- **Announcements** for new releases, new game sources and anything that changes how you
  set Orbit up.
- **Articles** about how Orbit works under the hood: Wine containers, the Steam client,
  controller navigation, graphics layers and the trade-offs behind them.

<Callout title="Prefer a feed?">
  Every post is also published in the [RSS feed](/blog/rss.xml), so you can follow along
  in any reader.
</Callout>

## A better changelog, too

The new [changelog](/changelog) gives every release its own page. Each one explains what
changed and why it matters, links every change to the code that made it (the commits and
pull requests between two tags) and shows how big the release was.

## Tell us what you think

Each post shows how many times it has been read, and you can react to it or share it on
X, Bluesky, LinkedIn, Reddit or Hacker News right from the end of the article. Orbit
counts views and reactions anonymously: there are no accounts and no trackers.

If something is missing or broken, [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/LFSCamargo/orbit/issues).
